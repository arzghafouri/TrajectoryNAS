Role & Context:
We are developing a Trajectory Prediction model based on the nuScenes dataset with a Supernet search (TrajectoryNAS) and Final Model architecture in PyTorch (`preprocessing.py`, `final_model.py`, `search_supernet.py`, `supernet_operators.py`).

Problem:
Currently, in `preprocessing.py`:
1. Stationary/low-motion target agents are filtered out ONLY from the validation split (`if split_name.lower() == "val" and not is_genuinely_moving(...)`), while the training split retains stationary targets. This introduces a major distribution mismatch and causes the model to optimize for trivial zero-velocity predictions on static targets during training.
2. Static obstacles and parked/stationary objects must act strictly as environmental context (neighbors/obstacles) in the Interaction branch, rather than target prediction goals.

Objective:
Align preprocessing across Train and Val splits uniformly:
- Remove stationary and low-motion agents from being selected as PRIMARY TARGETS in BOTH `Train` and `Val`.
- Ensure all stationary/parked vehicles and static obstacles remain fully accessible in the scene as NEIGHBOR / OBSTACLE tokens for the Interaction branch.
